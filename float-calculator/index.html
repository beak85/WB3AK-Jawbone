Model and assumptions

Float altitude is where air density equals total system mass (envelope + payload + gas) divided by the balloon's full volume. The atmosphere is the 1976 International Standard Atmosphere, valid from 0 to 32 km; real temperatures and pressures vary with season and latitude. Gas mass comes from total lift at launch, assuming pure gas at sea level (1.225 kg/m³, 15 °C). Overpressure at float is free lift divided by total system mass, with the gas at air temperature: sun heating adds several percent and night cooling removes a few. The volume is held fixed, so stretch under higher overpressure isn't modeled.
